## The Problem

Small businesses track expenses in a mix of paper receipts, email threads, and memory. When tax season arrives — or a client asks for a cost breakdown — there's no single place to look.

## The Solution

A focused expense tracker with one job: record every business cost and make it retrievable. Add an expense in seconds, attach the receipt, filter by category or month, and edit or delete any record from the detail view.

## Key Features

- **Receipt uploads** — attach a JPG, PNG, or PDF to any expense; stored securely and viewable inline
- **Category filter** — Food, Travel, Equipment, Software, Marketing, Other with colour-coded badges
- **Month filter** — narrow the list to any calendar month with a running total
- **Edit and delete** — open any expense to correct details or remove it entirely
- **Monthly total** — always-visible spend summary for the active month
